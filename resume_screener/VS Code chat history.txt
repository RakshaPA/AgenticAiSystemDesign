## Recruiter Side Workflow

The recruiter first logs into the platform and creates a new job opening by entering the job title, description, required skills, preferred skills, experience level, certifications, and scoring weights. The recruiter can also configure screening thresholds such as minimum similarity score, shortlist threshold, rejection threshold, and manual review threshold. Once the job is created, the system generates embeddings for the job description and stores them in PostgreSQL with pgvector or FAISS for fast semantic matching.

After creating the job role, the recruiter uploads resumes individually or in bulk through drag-and-drop, ZIP upload, LinkedIn import, email parsing, or GitHub profile import. As resumes are uploaded, the frontend shows real-time processing progress such as uploading, parsing, bias removal, embedding generation, similarity matching, scoring, and final decision generation.

---

# Backend Processing Workflow

Once a resume reaches the backend, the system extracts text from PDF or DOCX files and converts the resume into structured JSON format. The parser identifies sections such as skills, education, projects, certifications, work experience, and technical stack. After parsing, the bias removal module removes sensitive fields such as name, gender, age, date of birth, address, phone number, email, and other personal identifiers before evaluation begins.

The cleaned resume content is then converted into vector embeddings using an embedding model. The job description embeddings are already stored in the vector database. The system performs semantic similarity matching between resumes and job descriptions to quickly filter out unrelated applications. This prevents unnecessary LLM calls and improves scalability when handling thousands of resumes.

Only resumes that pass the similarity threshold move to the next stage. The scoring engine then evaluates candidates using a weighted scoring system. Different metrics contribute different percentages to the final score, such as required skills match, preferred skills match, years of experience, project relevance, certifications, and education quality. Each category contributes to the total weighted score.

Based on the final score:

* High-scoring candidates are automatically shortlisted
* Medium-scoring candidates are moved to a manual recruiter review queue
* Low-scoring candidates are automatically rejected

After the decision is generated, LLM agents are used only for explainability. The LLM generates recruiter-friendly explanations describing why the candidate was shortlisted, rejected, or sent for manual review. This keeps LLM usage minimal and cost-efficient.

All processing details including similarity scores, weighted scoring breakdown, AI explanations, recruiter overrides, and final decisions are stored in audit logs for transparency and compliance.

---

# Frontend Workflow

The recruiter dashboard displays real-time hiring analytics such as total applications, shortlisted candidates, rejected candidates, pending reviews, skill distributions, and hiring trends. Recruiters can open a candidate profile to see score breakdowns, semantic similarity visualization, project relevance, skill overlap, and AI-generated explanations.

Borderline candidates appear in the manual review queue where recruiters can compare profiles, add notes, override AI decisions, and finalize selections. The platform also includes a fairness dashboard showing how bias-related fields were removed and how evaluations were based only on relevant professional qualifications.

An audit dashboard provides complete visibility into every stage of processing including upload time, parsing stage, embedding generation, scoring, AI decisions, recruiter overrides, and final hiring outcomes.

---

# End-to-End System Flow

A recruiter creates a job role and uploads resumes. The backend extracts resume content, removes bias-sensitive information, and converts both resumes and job descriptions into embeddings. Semantic similarity filtering quickly eliminates irrelevant resumes. Relevant resumes then pass through the weighted scoring engine where candidates are ranked based on technical and professional fit. Highly relevant candidates are shortlisted, borderline candidates are sent for human review, and weak matches are rejected. LLMs generate final human-readable explanations, and all processing data is stored for analytics, explainability, and auditing.
